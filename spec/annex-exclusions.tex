\subsection{Reserved words}
\label{sec:reserved-words}

\begin{AddedBlock}
Python's soft keywords \pyinline{match}, \pyinline{case}, \pyinline{type} and \pyinline{_} are keywords only in their
statement or pattern positions and identifiers elsewhere, so that a Python program may bind a variable named
\pyinline{type}. \PurePy reserves them in every position (\secref{lexical}), anticipating their use as keywords in later
versions; \pyinline{_} is the wildcard pattern and never a variable.
\end{AddedBlock}
