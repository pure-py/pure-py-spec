Floor division of float $n$ by nonzero float $n'$, with the algorithm of the Python Language Reference, \S6.7, note~2. For $n$ and $n'$ other than $\nan$, let $x = \realOf(n)$ and $x' = \realOf(n')$. For finite $n$ and $n'$, let $x_1 = x - (x' \times \trunc(x / x'))$, the remainder of truncated division computed exactly, $n_2 = \fl(\realOf(\fl(x - x_1)) / x')$, and $n_3 = \fl(\realOf(n_2) - 1)$ if $x_1 \neq 0$ has the opposite sign to $x'$, and $n_2$ otherwise.
