The variables of the enclosing scope captured by lambdas within expression $e$; the generator variables of a
comprehension are local to it. The subscript distinguishes this from $\capturesS(t)$, since an expression
statement is both an expression and a statement. On the cases not shown it recurses structurally, taking the
union over the immediate subexpressions. The second form takes the union over a sequence of
expressions.
