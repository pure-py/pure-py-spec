Names declared anywhere in $t$, or bound by the imports of prefix $\seq{\iota}$, in textual order and with repeats, not descending into nested functions. \added{Plain imports with the same root, such as \pyinline{a.b} and \pyinline{a.c}, declare the root once.} It is empty on the statements not shown and on the empty prefix, and is the concatenation over the branches of a conditional and the cases of a match.
