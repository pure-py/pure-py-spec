Names declared anywhere in statement $t$, imports $\seq{\iota}$ or module body $\seq{\iota}\;t$, in textual order and with duplicates, not descending into nested functions. Plain imports with the same root, such as \pyinline{a.b} and \pyinline{a.c}, declare the root once. It is empty on the statements not shown and on the empty imports, and is the concatenation over the branches of a conditional and the cases of a match.
