The metafunction $\capturesS$ extends to blocks and
module bodies by union (like $\assignsS$), with the singleton block identified with its statement.
There $\fvE(e)$ and $\fvS(b)$ are the free variables of an expression and of a block, defined in the
usual way. On the cases not shown, $\binds$ and $\capturesE$ recurse structurally, taking the union over
immediate subterms, and $\capturesS$ takes the union of $\capturesE$ over a statement's subexpressions and
$\capturesS$ over the blocks it contains. The metafunction $\assignsS$ is empty on the statements not shown, is the union over the branch blocks of
a conditional, and does not descend into nested function definitions, which have their own scope.
