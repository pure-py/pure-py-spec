\section{Syntactic analyses}
\label{sec:mf-syntactic}

The analyses $\assignsS$, $\binds$, $\capturesS$ and $\capturesE$ introduced in \secref{wf-aux} are
defined by their principal cases; the remaining cases are stated with each.

\begin{auxfunction}{\assignsS(s)}{set of variables}{aux:assigns}
The variables assigned anywhere in $s$, not descending into nested function definitions, which have their
own scope. It is empty on the statements not shown and is the union over the branch blocks of a
conditional. It extends by union from statements to blocks, with the singleton block identified with its
statement, and on a module body $\seq{\iota}\;\seq{s}$ acts on $\seq{s}$.
\begin{align*}
\input{functions/assigns}
\end{align*}
\end{auxfunction}

\begin{auxfunction}{\binds(p)}{set of variables}{aux:binds}
The variables introduced by pattern $p$. On the cases not shown it recurses structurally, taking the union
over the immediate subpatterns.
\begin{align*}
\input{functions/binds}
\end{align*}
\end{auxfunction}

\begin{auxfunction}{\capturesS(s)}{set of variables}{aux:captures}
The variables of the enclosing scope captured by closures (lambdas or nested function definitions) within
$s$. On the cases not shown it takes the union of $\capturesE$ over the statement's subexpressions and of
$\capturesS$ over the blocks it contains, and it extends to blocks and module bodies as $\assignsS$ does.
\begin{align*}
\input{functions/captures}
\end{align*}
\end{auxfunction}

\begin{auxfunction}{\capturesE(e)}{set of variables}{aux:captures-e}
The variables of the enclosing scope captured by closures within expression $e$. On the cases not shown it
recurses structurally, taking the union over the immediate subexpressions.
\begin{align*}
\input{functions/captures-e}
\end{align*}
\end{auxfunction}

\begin{auxfunction}{\fvS(b), \fvE(e)}{set of variables}{aux:fv}
The free variables of a block and of an expression, defined in the usual way.
\end{auxfunction}
